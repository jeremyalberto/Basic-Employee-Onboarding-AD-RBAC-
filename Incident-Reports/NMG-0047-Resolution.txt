Ticket number: NMG-0047
Affected user: Jane Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)

Issue reported:
Unable to access HR shared resources. Desktop policies and mapped drives differ from teammates (different wallpaper, different software).

Investigation:
- Jane Cooper is currently in the Operations OU. She should be in the HR OU.
- Jane Cooper is currently a member of: Domain Users, Operations-Users. She should be a member of: Domain Users, HR-Users.

Root cause:
Two problems. First, Jane was placed in the wrong OU (Operations), so Operations policies were being applied to her, which is why her wallpaper and software were different from her HR peers. Second, she was in the wrong security group (Operations-Users instead of HR-Users), so she had Operations permissions and none of the HR permissions her role needs, which is why she got Access Denied on HR software and files.

Fix applied:
- Moved jcooper from the Operations OU to the HR OU so HR policies apply.
- Removed jcooper from Operations-Users.
- Added jcooper to HR-Users so she gets HR access.

Verification:
- Confirmed jcooper shows in the HR OU next to her HR teammates.
- Confirmed HR-Users is listed on her Member Of tab and she shows in the HR-Users members list.
- Confirmed with the user that HR policies are applied and she has the correct software and access.

Recommendation:
Improve the new hire provisioning process. Use a request form completed by department heads/managers (HR starts the process) to cut down on misassigned groups. Managers should only be able to request accounts for roles in their own department (ex. an HR manager could not request an account for an IT hire).
